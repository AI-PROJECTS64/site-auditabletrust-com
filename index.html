Terminology and scope

Auditable Trust describes systems where trust is not assumed: critical claims and behaviors can be independently verified from reliable evidence, with integrity protections and clear verification procedures. This is distinct from reputation-based trust and from “trust” claims that cannot be inspected. Auditable Trust is not a single technology and may be enabled by audit logs, transparency logs, attestations, cryptographic receipts, confidential computing and privacy-preserving verification.
